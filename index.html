What does Spotlight do?

Spotlight is a full-spectrum media marketing agency. We run digital marketing (Meta & Google Ads), public relations, creative production, influencer marketing, brand strategy, and AI-accelerated content automation for Blog, News and Events — all from one strategic core.

Is Spotlight's AI-generated content reviewed by a human before it publishes?

Yes, always. Spotlight uses agentic AI and automation tooling to draft calendars, copy, and content at speed, but nothing publishes without a human approval click. This guardrail applies to every client- and brand-facing deliverable.

Does Spotlight manage Meta and Google Ads campaigns?

Yes. Spotlight's Digital Marketing service covers Meta and Google Ads strategy, setup, and budget pacing, backed by always-on performance dashboards.

Can Spotlight automate a brand's Blog, News, and Events content?

Yes. Spotlight's Content & Automation service uses agentic AI and n8n pipelines to draft, queue, and route Blog, News, and Event content for human approval, structured for SEO, AEO, and GEO from day one.

What makes Spotlight different from a traditional marketing agency?

Spotlight pairs sharp human strategy with AI-accelerated execution across six disciplines under one intelligence layer, rather than treating each channel as a separate vendor relationship — with a human-approval gate on every AI-assisted output.
